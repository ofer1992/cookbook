Went out and made a Poyke dish. It's a very improvisatory thing, which I struggled with, or at least, I want to "get it right". We ended up using the following ingredients:
- Potatoes
- Sweet Potatoes
- Zucchini
- Onion
- Garlic
- Mushrooms
- Sweet Chestnut
- Some meat for Gulash
- Red wine
The onion we fried at first with some oil, then we also added the meat and fried it until it was brown from all sides, it's always the first step with stews and meat. We then added the spices and the water. I forgot to put the wine at first but added it as the water evaporated (and drank some). We also added the mushrooms and chestnuts later in the cooking.

We let it cook for about and hour in the fire. I don't know what's the Poyke's secret, but it gets a really good taste. The only problem was we started so late we only ate around 11pm, and the meat could have used more time.

Overall, a great and fun dish, but I still wanna see what a "real" Poyke is like.
