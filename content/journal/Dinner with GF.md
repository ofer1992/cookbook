Cooked dinner for GF. She asked for [[Minute Steak]], so I went with the Miznon-style minute steak in pita dish. Also added sides of [[Smashed Potatoes]] and [[Steamed Green Beans]]. Turned out pretty nice, she liked it. Still, I felt things weren't 100%:
- Even though the potatoes ended up tasty, the surface was a bit dried-out, maybe because I left it in the oven? not sure
- I made the green beans in the microwave, it also didn't turn out perfectly. The beans were frozen and themselves a bit wonky, so maybe that's to blame.
- The minute steak was alright, but I have no idea how it's "supposed" to be.

I think the reason behind it was that I focus on the steak as the main "new" thing and winged the other dishes, while in truth I haven't mastered them yet.
