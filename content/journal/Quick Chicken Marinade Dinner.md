I bought groceries today, among them chicken breast and yoghurt, and I figured I'd do a [[Yoghurt Lemon Garlic Marinated Chicken]]. I asked gemini for a recipe, was too lazy to google it myself. This is the that I linked to (as of the time of writing). I didn't follow it completely;
- Didn't premix the base
- Didn't add lemon zest
- Didn't add paprika or dried oregano, also didn't follow the amounts, went by feel
- Sliced the breast somewhat crudely
- Didn't pat the chicken dry
- Marinated almost 30 minutes
- Use a Pan on high heat, at first without oil, then added.
- didn't shake the marinade off the chicken.

It came out alright but not super tasty, also I ate it on crackers with dips so it kinda took over the taste. Still, I remember I've done it in the past and it was better. Some things to try next time:
- Follow the missing instructions.
- Use the oven.
