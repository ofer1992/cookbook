Based on the amount of chicken in your container (looks like roughly 1.5 to 2 lbs or ~800g), here is a perfect **Lemon Garlic Yogurt Marinade** recipe.

### The Ingredients

**The Base:**
*   **Yogurt:** ½ cup plain yogurt (Greek yogurt is best because it’s thick and sticks to the meat, but regular works too).
*   **Lemon:** 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (about half a lemon) + **Lemon Zest** (grate the yellow skin before juicing; this is where the real lemon aroma lives).
*   **Garlic:** 3 to 4 cloves, minced or crushed (add more if you love garlic!).
*   **Olive Oil:** 1 tablespoon (helps conduct the heat when cooking).

**The Seasonings (The "Mediterranean" Style):**
*   **Salt:** 1 teaspoon.
*   **Black Pepper:** ½ teaspoon.
*   **Dried Oregano:** 1 teaspoon (classic pairing with lemon).
*   **Paprika:** ½ teaspoon (adds a nice color so the chicken isn't too pale).

### Instructions

1.  **Prep the Chicken:** If you haven't already, you might want to slice those larger breasts into even cutlets or bite-sized chunks. This ensures they cook evenly. **Tip:** Pat the chicken dry with a paper towel first; this helps the marinade stick better.
2.  **Mix:** In a small bowl, whisk together the yogurt, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, oil, and spices.
3.  **Combine:** Pour the mixture over the chicken right in that plastic container. Use your hands or a spoon to toss it until every piece is heavily coated.
4.  **Marinate:**
    *   **Minimum:** 30 minutes (on the counter).
    *   **Ideal:** 2 to 4 hours (in the fridge).
    *   *Note:* You can leave it overnight, but try not to go over 24 hours or the texture might get a little too soft.

### How to Cook It

Since yogurt marinades can burn easily (because of the milk solids), here is the best way to cook it:

*   **Pan-Sear (Stovetop):** Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat with a little oil. Shake off the *excess* marinade from the chicken (don't rinse it, just shake off the globs). Cook for about 5–7 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through.
*   **Oven (Baking):** Preheat to 400°F (200°C). Place chicken on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Bake for 20–25 minutes.
*   **Grilling:** Keep the heat medium (not high). Oil the grates well. Grill for about 5–6 minutes per side.

**Optional add-in:** If you want a warmer "Shawarma" flavor, add ½ teaspoon of **Cumin** and a pinch of **Cinnamon** to the mix.
